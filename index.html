I am a direct Ph.D. student at the Zhan Tianyou Honors College, Beijing Jiaotong University. My current research focuses on compositional image retrieval, with broader interests in computer vision, multimodal representation learning, and vision-language retrieval.

I am interested in building retrieval systems that understand fine-grained visual concepts and compositional user intent, especially when queries combine text, images, attributes, and relational constraints.

Research

My work centers on compositional image retrieval: retrieving target images according to structured user intent such as object attributes, scene context, visual changes, and image-text composition. I am especially interested in robust visual-language alignment, fine-grained representation learning, and efficient retrieval for large-scale image collections.

  • Compositional image retrieval and image-text matching.
  • Multimodal representation learning for fine-grained visual understanding.
  • Efficient indexing and ranking for large-scale visual search.
